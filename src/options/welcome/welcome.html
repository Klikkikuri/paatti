What marks do you want to see?

Marks sit next to a headline. Switch each one on or off. Klikkikuri works with all of them off.

By default, only the AI mark is on.

Where should Klikkikuri work?

Some sites work right away. Others ask your browser for permission first.

If your browser asks and you say no, the site stays off. Everything else keeps working, so you can continue without it.

Your site is not here? Request it on GitHub.

What if a rewrite is wrong?

Every rewrite has a feedback card with two choices. Feedback is sent only when you press a button there.

  1. 1
    Is goodOne click tells Klikkikuri that the rewrite is fine. It is sent at once.
  2. 2
    Is no goodSay what is wrong, or write a better headline. Then press Submit. The card needs some text.

To open the card, click the mark on a rewritten headline, if you switched it on. You can also use the feedback list in the toolbar popup.

How do you get new fixes?

Klikkikuri brings new fixes to your browser by itself. You can choose how often it checks.

The maintainers use feedback to check how accurate the rewrites are, so your feedback can lead to better fixes later.
