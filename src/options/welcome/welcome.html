Welcome to Klikkikuri Paatti

Klikkikuri Paatti is a browser extension that replaces clickbait headlines with neutral headlines on supported news sites. You will not notice Klikkikuri Paatti when it works. Your blood pressure will notice when it stops.

  • The service writes the headlinesThe Klikkikuri service reads each new article on the front pages of supported sites. AI decides if the headline is clickbait and writes a new one when needed.
  • Your reading stays on your deviceThe Klikkikuri Paatti browser extension checks each headline against a list on your device. Nothing about what you read goes to the service unless you send feedback.
  • Free as in beer, free as in freedomThe basic version is free as in beer: it costs nothing. The code is free as in freedom: the Klikkikuri Paatti browser extension and the service are open source under the EUPL-1.2 license. A planned paid version will also fix headlines behind a paywall.

Where do the new headlines come from?

  1. 1
    Reads the newsIt reads each new article on the front pages of supported sites in full.
  2. 2
    Writes a better headlineA language model rates the clickbait, writes a new headline if needed, and notes any AI smell.
  3. 3
    Publishes a listNew headlines go into a public list, each with the fingerprint of its article.
iHow the Klikkikuri service writes a headline
  1. It reads the whole article, not only the headline.
  2. It checks if the original headline is clickbait.
  3. It writes a new headline by these rules:
    • Says who does what, in plain active words.
    • Gives an opinion or a forecast to the one who made it.
    • Names the role of the speaker, and says if the source has an interest in the matter.
    • Uses neutral words. No drama, no hook.
Example

OriginalThe economy snaps: This will ruin Finland!

RewrittenEmployers' lobby group says workers earn too much

The original makes a lobby group sound like the whole economy.

What happens in your browser?

  1. 4
    Gets the listKlikkikuri Paatti downloads the list at intervals and keeps it on your device.
  2. 5
    Fixes the pageThe extension matches the fingerprint of each link against the list on your device. Nothing you read leaves your browser unless you send feedback.
  3. 6
    Your feedbackSent only when you press send. A disputed headline is written again while the article is on a front page.

What marks do you want to see?

Marks sit next to a headline. Switch each one on or off. Klikkikuri works with all of them off.

Where should Klikkikuri work?

Some sites work right away. Others ask your browser for permission first.

If your browser asks and you say no, the site stays off. Everything else keeps working, so you can continue without it.

What does the toolbar button show?

Click the Klikkikuri Paatti button in the toolbar to see what the extension did on the page you are on.

Pin Klikkikuri Paatti for easier access: open the puzzle-piece menu in the toolbar and select the pin.

Pin Klikkikuri Paatti for easier access: open the extensions button in the toolbar and choose Pin to Toolbar.

To find Klikkikuri Paatti, open the Firefox menu and choose Extensions.

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

What if a rewrite is wrong, or clickbait gets through?

Every rewrite has a feedback card with two choices. Feedback is sent only when you press a button there.

  1. 1
    Is goodOne click tells the Klikkikuri service that the rewrite is fine. It is sent at once.
  2. 2
    Is no goodSay what is wrong, or write a better headline. Then press Submit. The card needs some text.

To open the card, click the mark on a rewritten headline, if you switched it on. You can also use the feedback list in the toolbar popup.

Clickbait that gets through Headlines that your strictness setting leaves alone have a rewrite too. At the bottom of the feedback list in the popup, a button shows them, and you can rate them like any other rewrite.

iWhy do I still see clickbait?
  • The list updates at intervals, so the newest headlines wait for the next update.
  • News sites sometimes put older articles on their front pages. The Klikkikuri service reads only new articles, so these keep their headline.
  • An article page keeps its own headline. Klikkikuri Paatti rewrites only the links to it.
  • Articles behind a paywall keep their headline in the free version.
  • Klikkikuri Paatti works only on the supported sites, and not on every topic.
